Agreement and company policy

Landlord Partnership Agreement

1. Parties. This agreement is between Finder ("the Company") and the landlord registering on this form ("the Landlord").

2. Marketing services. The Company will list and market the Landlord's property to prospective tenants across its platform.

3. Commission. The Landlord agrees to pay the Company a commission of 8% of the rent for each tenancy secured through Finder.

4. Rent holding period. To protect all parties from fraud, the Company will hold the first rent payment made by a tenant for 48 hours. After this period, and provided no dispute has been raised, the rent less commission is released to the Landlord's registered bank account.

5. Accuracy of listings. The Landlord confirms that all information and documents supplied are true and that they have the legal right to let the property.

6. Termination. Either party may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice. Commission remains payable on tenancies already secured.

Company Policy

Honest listings. Photos, prices and amenities must match the property as it is. Misleading listings will be removed.

Fair treatment. Landlords must not discriminate against tenants on the basis of tribe, religion, gender, disability or any other protected ground.

Payments through Finder. Deposits and rent for tenants found through Finder must be paid through the platform. Asking tenants to pay outside the platform is a breach of policy.

Property condition. The property must be safe, habitable and fit for the purpose advertised.

Data protection. Tenant and landlord personal data may only be used for the purpose of letting the property.

Enforcement. Breach of this policy may lead to suspension or removal from the platform.
